## ReportForge (2026 – present, independent build)
- Problem: AI can write a financial report in seconds but sometimes invents numbers. One made-up figure ruins trust in the whole report.
- What it does: every number the AI writes is checked against the source data before the report goes out. Four checks per figure: is it sourced, is anything missing, is the math right, are the names right. Unverifiable numbers are flagged or blocked. Each report gets a 0–100 trust score.
- Reports: flux analysis (why numbers changed), budget vs. actual, price-volume-mix (what drove revenue up or down).
- Product decision: started with dozens of report types, then narrowed to three finance reports once the verification engine was proven. Darryn calls this the hardest and best decision on the project.
- How it was built: solo, with AI as the developer and Darryn as product manager. Ticketed backlog, a Strategist → Critic → Planner review step for major design decisions, roughly 3,000 automated tests, a test harness that deliberately tries to trick the AI into making things up (the build fails if the trust score drops), and security reviews.
- Built with: React, Node, Supabase, Claude API, Vercel and Railway.
